Retraction Reach
Find the retracted papers a reference list relies on, up to two citations deep. For authors, reviewers and editors.
Source

Real reference lists of published papers, already expanded two citation layers and stored in a Relate graph.

Your browser asks Crossref for each paper's reference list, then for the reference lists of those papers. A 40-item list takes about a minute. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Where the data comes from
  • Retractions: Retraction Watch database, published by Crossref under CC0.
  • Who cites whom: Crossref reference lists.
  • Citation intent (method, result, background): Semantic Scholar, bundled lists only.

A retraction is not always misconduct; many are honest corrections. A citation to a retracted paper can also be a critique of it. Read the path before you judge.

Starting…

Click a row to see the citation path. Scores follow a fixed rule: a retracted reference is 100; citing a retracted paper directly starts at 70 (+20 if cited after the retraction notice, +10 if cited for its method or result); two citations away starts at 30. 60 and above is marked Check.
